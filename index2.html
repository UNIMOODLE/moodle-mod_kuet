What is KUET?

KUET is a Moodle activity module for interactive quizzes, polls and competitions using the question bank. Teachers can lead a live session or let students work at their own pace, individually or in teams.

Who is it for?

For teachers, trainers and students using Moodle who want interactive participation and assessment in their courses. KUET is compatible with Moodle 4.1 (from 4.1.2), 4.2 and 4.3–4.5. Moodle 5.0 is not yet supported because of the changes to the question bank system. Check which version to install.

Why use KUET?

Reuse Moodle question bank content and prepare multiple sessions within one activity.

Combine manual or scheduled progression with inactive, podium and race modes. Configure timers, rankings and feedback to suit your learning goals.

Review session, participant and question reports, and download them in the formats enabled on your platform.

KUET is open-source software developed through UNIMOODLE.

Features

Designed for Moodle

KUET integrates with Moodle’s question bank, gradebook and activity completion. Its interface adapts to mobile devices.

Part of your virtual campus

Teachers and students work in their familiar platform, using Moodle roles, courses and groupings.

Feedback and reports

Show answers, feedback and statistics during manual sessions. Once a session ends, review results by session, participant and question to identify successes and difficulties.

Game modes

Choose teacher-led manual progression or self-paced scheduled progression. Combine it with inactive, podium or race mode, and participate individually or in teams. Explore the six session modes.
