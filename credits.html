A UNIMOODLE project

Universities supporting the Moodle community

A community project

In October 2022, the universities of Valladolid, Complutense de Madrid, País Vasco/EHU, León, Salamanca, Illes Balears, Valencia, Rey Juan Carlos, La Laguna, Zaragoza, Málaga, Córdoba, Extremadura, Vigo, Las Palmas and Burgos formed an inter-university group to improve Moodle-based virtual campuses, supported by European NextGenerationEU funding through the UNIDIGITAL programme.

Developed by Tresipunt

UNIMOODLE selected development teams through a competitive call to build new components for the Moodle community. KUET was developed by Tresipunt, an educational technology company and Moodle partner.
